## Who builds the house
The building is built by Chinor Development, a company from Tashkent. Chinor Park is its first project on the Domus Estate platform.

## Good to know
The Chinor Park complex and the developer Chinor Development are fictional: this is a study project. Bookings, payments and documents on the site are for testing and create no real obligations.
