## What kind of building
A comfort-class building: 12 floors and 92 apartments on floors 2 to 12. The ground floor holds the entrance lobby and shared areas.

## Which apartments
Layouts range from studios of about 29 m² to four-room apartments of up to 97 m². The building has two elevators.
