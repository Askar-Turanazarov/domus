## What is done
As of October 2026 the excavation and the foundation are finished, and the monolithic frame of the building is under construction.

## What comes next
The next stages are completing the frame of all 12 floors, the facade and glazing in the first half of 2027, utilities and landscaping of the yard by autumn 2027, and then acceptance of the building. The manager sends a photo report of the work on request.
