## The steps
The steps are: choosing an apartment, booking, a viewing if you wish, signing the agreement at the sales office, payment, and accepting the apartment and receiving the keys after the handover.

## How booking works
First the apartment is held for 15 minutes while you pay the deposit. After the deposit is paid the booking lasts 72 hours; it can be extended by agreement with the manager.
