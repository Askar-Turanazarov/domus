## What the deposit is for
The deposit secures the apartment for you during the booking: nobody else can buy it in that time. When you buy, the deposit counts toward the price of the apartment.

## Amount and refund
The amount is shown on the booking page; in the chat it is given by the calculation tool, not by the knowledge-base consultant. The refund conditions are set out in the offer.
