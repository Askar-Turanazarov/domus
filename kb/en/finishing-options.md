## Which levels exist
Apartments come in three options: rough finish (bare walls and screed), pre-finish (white box: levelled walls and utility preparation) and full finish (ready to move in).

## How to see it
You can see how an apartment looks with different finishes in 3D on the site. Ask the manager for the exact finishing level of a particular apartment.
