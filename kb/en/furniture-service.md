## The 3D planner
The site has a 3D planner: you can open an apartment layout, place furniture, look at the rooms in 3D and walk through them.

## Ordering furniture
You can send your arrangement as a request for custom furniture: prices in the planner are approximate, and the exact cost is given after measurement.
