## When the building is handed over
The Chinor Park building (12 floors) is handed over in the fourth quarter of 2027. The date is approximate: it depends on the progress of construction, and the manager confirms the current date.

## After the handover
Once the building is commissioned, apartments are accepted: buyers are invited to an inspection, sign the acceptance act and then receive the keys. Acceptance takes place within 60 days after the building is commissioned.
