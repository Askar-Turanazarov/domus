## How the installment works
The installment is provided by the developer without a bank. A down payment of at least 30 % of the apartment price is required, and the rest is split into equal monthly payments over 12 to 36 months.

## Markup
The longer the term, the higher the markup on the remainder. The installment calculator in the chat or on the site gives the exact figures for a specific apartment.
