Public offer for booking an apartment

Edition of 27.10.2026

Chinor Development LLC (hereinafter, the Developer) invites any person (hereinafter, the Client) to book an apartment in the Chinor Park residential complex on the terms below. Paying the deposit means agreeing to the offer and the refund terms.

1. Subject

  • The Developer reserves the chosen free apartment for the Client for the booking period, and the Client pays a deposit.
  • A booking is not a sale contract. The contract is signed separately at the sales office.

2. The deposit and how it counts

  • The deposit is a fixed sum, the same for every apartment: 10 000 000 UZS. The current sum is always shown on the booking page before payment.
  • On purchase the deposit counts towards the price of the apartment, and with an installment plan towards the first payment.
  • All sums are in Uzbek sum (UZS).

3. Hold and booking period

  • Once an apartment is chosen, it is held for the Client while they pay — 15 minutes. If the deposit is not paid, the hold is released automatically.
  • After payment the booking lasts 72 hours. During this time the Client comes to the sales office to sign the contract.
  • One Client can have no more than two active bookings.
  • All times are Tashkent time (UTC+5).

4. Extension

  • Only a manager or the head of sales of the Developer can extend a booking, with a reason. The Client may ask for an extension in the chat, in Telegram or by phone.
  • The longest single extension is set in the Developer’s settings and communicated to the Client.

5. A booking that ends without a deal

  • If the booking period is over and no contract is signed, the booking expires automatically and the apartment becomes free again. The Client is notified.

6. Deposit refund

  • If the booking expires or the Client withdraws from it, the deposit is not refunded. The Developer’s head of sales may refund it manually, stating the reason.
  • If the Developer cancels the booking (for example, because of a price error or because the apartment is withdrawn from sale), the deposit is refunded in full automatically.
  • A payment that arrives after the hold has ended creates no booking and is refunded to the Client in full.
  • Only the Developer’s head of sales makes a refund; the money goes back the same way it was paid.

7. Payment methods

  • UzCard or HUMO bank card, Click, an international card via Stripe.
  • In the training version every method works in test mode: no real money is charged, only test cards are accepted.
  • The Developer does not store card data.

8. Cancellation

  • Until the deposit is paid, the Client can release the apartment on the booking page — free of charge.
  • The Client can cancel a paid booking on the booking page or through a manager; the deposit then stays with the Developer (section 6).
  • The Developer can cancel a booking, stating a reason; when the Developer is at fault, the deposit is refunded (section 6).

9. Furniture order prepayment

  • For a furniture order from the Domus planner the Client may make a prepayment of 30% of the order estimate. A manager confirms the dates and the final price; the rest is paid after delivery and assembly.
  • If the order is cancelled, the Developer’s head of sales refunds the prepayment on the Client’s request.

10. Contacts

  • Sales office: Tashkent (training address), Monday to Saturday, 9:00–18:00.
  • Phone: +998 90 000 00 00 (training number). The website chat and the Telegram bot are available 24/7.
