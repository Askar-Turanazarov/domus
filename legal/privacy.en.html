Privacy policy

Edition of 27.10.2026

This policy explains what data about you the Chinor Park website and Telegram bot collect, why, and how long it is kept. We keep the minimum needed for a booking and for contacting you.

1. What data we collect

  • Your name and phone number — when you book an apartment, sign up for a viewing or leave a request.
  • Your Telegram ID and language — if you write to the bot or open the mini app.
  • The history of your conversation with the AI consultant in the website chat and in Telegram.
  • Bookings, payments (sum, method, status) and furniture orders. We never receive or store bank card data: the test payment forms accept test numbers only, Stripe processes payments on its own page.

2. Why

  • To reserve an apartment for you, accept the payment, and remind you about the booking deadline and the viewing.
  • So that a manager can continue a conversation started with the AI consultant without asking the same questions again.
  • For anonymous sales statistics (funnel, conversion) — without names or phone numbers.

3. Where it is stored

  • In a Neon (PostgreSQL) database in the European Union, Frankfurt region (eu-central-1). The website runs on Vercel.
  • Only sales staff can access the data, according to their roles; actions on bookings and payments are logged.

4. What goes to AI providers

  • Chat answers are prepared by an external AI provider (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude). It receives the text of the conversation — without your phone number.
  • The training version uses test data only. On the free tier Google may use requests to improve its models — do not write anything in the chat you are not ready to share.

5. How long we keep it

  • Data is kept while it is needed for a booking, a deal or an order, and no longer than the training project lasts. The demo test data is deleted after the defence.

6. How to delete your data

  • Write to a manager in the chat or in Telegram, or call +998 90 000 00 00 (training number) — we will delete your data, or anonymise it if it is needed for payment records.

7. Cookies and browser storage

  • dvid — a technical cookie: a random visitor number used to count visits to the site. The counter keeps only a hash of it, without the IP address. If you book an apartment, the number is saved in your client record, so we can see how many visitors reach a booking. It lasts 365 days.
  • dcl — a technical cookie: it remembers you as a client after a booking, a viewing or a request, to show “My bookings”. It lasts 180 days. The “This is not me” button deletes it.
  • Browser storage (localStorage) keeps the language, theme, 3D quality and planner projects — on your device only.
  • There are no advertising or third-party cookies, so there is no cookie banner either.

8. Contacts

  • Chinor Development LLC, Tashkent (training project). Phone: +998 90 000 00 00 (training number).
